Policies

Terms & Conditions: Service availability, charges and estimated delivery are subject to courier partner rules. Customers must provide accurate sender, receiver and parcel information. Restricted and prohibited items will not be accepted.

Privacy Policy: Customer information is used to process courier requests, communicate booking updates and support delivery. Information may be shared with logistics partners only as needed to provide the service. Access is restricted to authorized personnel.

Cancellation: Cancellation requests must be sent to support before the parcel is handed to the courier partner. After pickup or shipment processing, cancellation may not be possible and partner charges may apply.

Refunds: Eligible refunds, if approved, will be initiated to the original payment method after payment reconciliation. Processing time depends on the payment provider and bank. Contact support with the booking code.
